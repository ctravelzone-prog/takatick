Frequently asked questions

Where does the customer's money go?

Straight to your own bKash / Nagad / Rocket / Upay personal number. {{BRAND}} never holds, touches or routes your money — it only reads the payment SMS and verifies TrxID + amount.

Can one TrxID pay twice?

No. One TrxID can be confirmed exactly once. A second attempt with the same TrxID is rejected (ALREADY_USED).

Who approves my package, and how long does it take?

Approval is manual — the admin checks your bKash payment (TrxID + amount) and approves the order. Once approved, your package is active for 30 days and you can see it in your dashboard.

What happens when my package expires?

Your package runs for 30 days from approval. After expiry you get a 3-day grace period — verification keeps working while you renew. After the grace period ends, verification stops until you renew. Your API keys and data are never deleted — renewal reuses the same keys.

Can I get a refund?

Before approval, ask the admin to cancel your pending order. Once approved and the package is active, the monthly fee is not refundable — please check the package facilities before paying.

Does it work without the SMS app?

No. The SMS app is what brings the payment SMS to the server. Without it, there is nothing to verify against. Download it from your dashboard after signing up.
