Frequently asked questions

Where does the customer's money go?

Straight to your own bKash / Nagad / Rocket / Upay personal number. {{BRAND}} never holds, touches or routes your money — it only verifies the payment.

Can one TrxID pay twice?

No. One TrxID can be confirmed exactly once — a second attempt with the same TrxID is rejected.

Who approves my package, and how long does it take?

Payment confirm howar sathe sathe package active hoye jay 30 diner jonno — dashboard-e dekhte parben. Prothom order-e ektu somoy lagte pare.

What happens when my package expires?

Your package runs for 30 days from approval. After expiry you get a 3-day grace period — verification keeps working while you renew. After the grace period ends, verification stops until you renew. Your API keys and data are never deleted — renewal reuses the same keys.

Can I get a refund?

Before approval, ask the admin to cancel your pending order. Once approved and the package is active, the monthly fee is not refundable — please check the package facilities before paying.

Does it work without the SMS app?

No — the SMS app is required. Download it from your dashboard after signing up.
